Nanvi Robotics portfolio

Four robots, four areas of impact

Product capabilities below describe Nanvi’s portfolio direction. Photos are illustrative examples and may not depict Nanvi-built prototypes.

SMART AGRICULTURE

Agri Bot

Designed for tea estates and plantations, Agri Bot uses computer vision to identify and remove weeds while protecting tea plants. Terrain-aware navigation is intended for sloping gardens, with operational reporting.

Discuss Agri Bot
DISABILITY ASSISTANCE

Care Bot

Designed to support independence in hospitals, rehabilitation centers, and care facilities with voice or touch interaction, small-item fetching, reminders, caregiver alerts, and basic indoor navigation.

Safety: Care Bot does not lift or transfer people, administer medication, or make medical decisions. Clinical care remains with qualified professionals.

Discuss Care Bot
INDUSTRIAL AUTOMATION

Indus Bot

Designed for AI visual inspection, defect detection, lightweight material movement, and machine-status monitoring. Dashboard views, maintenance alerts, and inspection reports support production visibility.

Discuss Indus Bot
EVENTS & HOSPITALITY

Event Bot

Designed for conferences, exhibitions, and hospitality venues: greet guests, support check-in and wayfinding, share information, and assist with lead capture and event analytics.

Planned capabilities include multilingual interaction and CRM integration.
